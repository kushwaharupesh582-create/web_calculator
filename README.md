# 🌈 Calculator – Aura Dynamic

A visually stunning, feature‑packed web calculator built with **pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.  
It combines a beautiful glassmorphism design with dynamic rainbow and gold colour animations, a fully editable expression line with a visible cursor, scrollable calculation history, and support for both basic and scientific operations.

![Calculator Preview](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400?text=Calculator+Preview+Coming+Soon)

## ✨ Features

- **Live Dynamic Background**  
  – Floating ambient orbs with shifting rainbow and gold hues that pulse smoothly.  
  – Mouse‑tracking outside glow light that follows your cursor.

- **Fully Editable Expression**  
  – Click anywhere in the expression line to place the blinking text cursor.  
  – Type numbers, operators, or scientific functions directly, and edit at any position.  
  – Buttons insert characters exactly where the caret is, not just at the end.

- **Basic & Scientific Modes**  
  – Toggle between *Basic* and *Scientific* (Trig & Log) with an animated slider.  
  – Scientific functions: `sin`, `cos`, `tan` (in **degrees**), `log`, `ln`, `√`, `x^y`, `π`.

- **Chain Calculations**  
  – After pressing `=` , pressing an operator automatically uses the last answer as the starting value – just like a physical calculator.

- **Scrollable History**  
  – Past calculations are stored inside the display.  
  – Scroll up to review your history; new results auto‑scroll to the bottom, so the display never expands.

- **Keyboard Support**  
  – Full number pad, operators (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`), `Enter` to calculate, `Backspace` to delete, `Esc` to clear all.

- **Tactile Ripple & Animations**  
  – Every button has a ripple effect on click.  
  – Results “bump” when updated.  
  – Glassmorphism card with golden borders and glow shadows.

- **Live Clock**  
  – A real‑time clock with seconds displayed at the top.

- **Responsive Design**  
  – Optimised for both desktop and mobile devices.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** – Semantic structure with contenteditable expression.  
- **CSS3** – Custom properties (CSS variables), animations, backdrop‑filter glass effect, smooth transitions, rainbow gradients.  
- **Vanilla JavaScript** – No frameworks or libraries. All logic is handwritten.
